## Long version (reply to the X post, or Reddit r/comfyui)

I rebuilt Higgsfield as an open-source app on ComfyUI. Meet OpenSfield.

- Same layout: Explore, Image, Video, Audio, Cinema Studio, Soul, Viral, Marketing, Apps
- All 83 Higgsfield Apps, wired: one tap for Nano Banana / Seedance, one tap for the open-weights version
- 146 tools: open-weight workflows (LTX, Wan, Qwen, FLUX) plus one tool per partner model (Seedance, Kling, GPT Image, Nano Banana...)
- Cinema Studio with camera, lens, 33 moves, palettes and lighting, @Elements for consistent characters, a Creator Hub of tutorials
- 130 tools and apps have real renders; the app examples were made through the app itself on one local GPU, open weights, no credits
- Runs on Comfy Cloud or your own ComfyUI. MIT.
