## What an element is

An element is a named reference: a **Character**, **Location**, **Prop**, **Style** or **Product**, with one or more images. Elements live on the **Elements** page (top bar) and in Cinema Studio under **My elements**.

## Create one

1. Open **Elements** and press **+ New element**. Pick the type first (Character, Location...).
2. Rename it to something short you will type, like `Maya`.
3. Press **+ Add image** on the card to add more angles: front, side, full body. More angles, better consistency.

You can also press **★** on any generation in History to turn it into an element.

## Use it

- Type **@** in any prompt box and pick the element. `@Maya` goes into the prompt and her cover image fills the tool's image input.
- Or press **From assets** under any image input.

## Tips

- Use plain backgrounds for character references. Busy backgrounds leak into the result.
- Keep one element per idea. "Maya in red coat" and "Maya in uniform" can be two elements.
- Locations work best as wide shots taken in the light you want.
